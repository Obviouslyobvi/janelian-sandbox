FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Joining is 100% free, and a legit study will never ask you to pay to participate. You only ever get paid.

How do I get paid?

The research company pays you directly, usually by PayPal, check, or gift card, after you complete the study.

Do I need any experience?

None. Companies specifically want everyday people and honest, unfiltered opinions.

How much can I make?

It varies. Focus groups often pay $50 to $250; surveys and online studies pay less but take less time. Think steady side income, not a full-time paycheck.

How often are there opportunities?

New studies come up regularly. The more complete your profile, the more often you'll get matched.
