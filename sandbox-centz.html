Sandbox Centz

Sharing is caring. Put our cents together to help members raise money and grow their small businesses.

FUNdraising

Raising money for a cause, a 5k, or a school fundraiser? Refer 3 new people to the Sandbox and Janel will blast your donation link to the entire database.

  • Your fundraiser link is shared once all 3 referrals Zoom and ID check
  • Great for pledges, donations, and school or club fundraisers
  • No GoFundMe links, please

Small business shout-outs

Small-business owners can share their passion too, one blast per month plus a link to your product or service on the Sandbox, per 3 referrals. It's free, and it puts you in front of over 1,000 members across the US with one click.

There's never a fee to be part of the Sandbox. Referrals are simply how Janel keeps growing the community and the paid opportunities.

Ready to say hello?

The Sandbox is invite-only and always free. Request an invite and Janel will be in touch.
